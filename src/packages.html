Frequently Asked Questions

What happens in a trial lesson?▼
We'll have a relaxed conversation about your goals, your current level, and why Korean matters to you. I'll check your pronunciation, grammar awareness, and listening comprehension through natural dialogue. Then I'll sketch a realistic plan for your success. Even if you choose a different tutor, you'll walk away with something valuable.
How fast will I improve?▼
It depends on your starting point, goals, and consistency. Most students on the Builder track notice clear improvement within 4-6 weeks. The key isn't lesson hours; it's what happens between classes. My system is designed to maximize your growth time, not just our meeting time.
Can I switch tracks?▼
Absolutely. Life changes, and your learning pace should too. Busy week? Go Light. Exam coming up? Switch to Accelerator. Just let me know and I'll adjust your plan immediately.
What if I miss a class?▼
24-hour cancellation policy. Life happens, and I respect that. If you need to reschedule, just message me. I'm flexible because I know consistency matters more than perfection.
What makes you different from other tutors?▼
I don't just teach Korean. I build a system for you. Personalized curriculum, daily micro-tasks, detailed feedback within 24 hours, and between-class support. My goal is to make myself unnecessary. I want you to eventually fly on your own.
Do I need any prior knowledge?▼
No. Complete beginners are welcome. I'll teach you Hangul (the Korean alphabet) in our first few sessions. If you already know some Korean, great, we'll find your exact level and build from there.
