This is a story about a teacher.

Not the kind who just explains grammar and disappears. A different kind.

🏆

The Coach

In Korea, I ran a learning center. Out of 450,000 tutors on the country's largest platform, I was ranked 170th.

Top 0.03%

📞

The Extra Mile

I didn't just teach. Late at night, I talked to worried parents. I convinced anxious mothers that rankings weren't everything — that their child's health and happiness mattered more.

😔

The Weight

I carried every student's worries. Their mentality, their fears, their family pressures. More than any tutor should.

Because I genuinely cared.

💨

March 2025

I stepped away. Completely burnt out.

Not from working too hard — from caring too much.

For the first time in my life, I just started traveling. No plan. No deadline. Just me and a backpack.

The Spark

“This is what I'm meant to do.”

The Videos

The Ignorance

People who never tried to understand. They judged from their narrow view and spoke out loud to the world. Their ignorance became someone else's “knowledge.”

The Bigger Problem

I saw it everywhere. Not just about Korea. People worldwide — thinking in black and white. Not trying to understand each other.

🔥

The Mission

I will protect the real Korea. And build something that helps people understand each other better.

I met students who had been made to feel ashamed by their previous tutors. Their confidence was broken before they even began.

The Promise

You deserve more than a chat partner. You deserve someone who takes your goals as seriously as you do.

Culture — The Context for Everything

  • Why Koreans ask about age — hierarchy is a respect system, not a power play
  • Why addresses go large to small — the collective comes before the individual
  • Nunchi (눈치) — reading the room before you speak a word
  • Language without culture is just noise

Structure — The Framework Within Culture

  • Korean word order: Subject-Object-Verb — the action always comes last
  • Sentence building blocks that stack like LEGO
  • 35-day plans, monthly roadmaps, daily micro-tasks
  • Random studying = random results

Sounds — What You Ultimately Produce

  • Pronunciation that native speakers actually understand
  • Intonation patterns that carry meaning beyond words
  • Self-correction techniques for independence
  • Your voice becomes your own — not a textbook echo

How It Actually Works

Phase 1: The Trial

Tell me your story, not just your grammar level. We talk about your life, your goals, and why Korean matters to you. Then I sketch a realistic plan.

Phase 2: The Roadmap

A specific plan — monthly, weekly, daily. Not “try harder” but “do this, then this, then verify here.”

Phase 3: Between Classes

Small assignments, voice recordings, gentle nudges. Detailed feedback within 24 hours — not just “great job!” but a thorough analysis with my own voice recordings.

Phase 4: In Class

By the time class starts, you've already improved. Class becomes a Growth Verification Session — not a lecture. You speak, I listen and refine.

Phase 5: Independence

The goal is for you to not need me. How to analyze sentences yourself, use AI effectively, self-correct pronunciation. When you can fly solo, my job is done.
