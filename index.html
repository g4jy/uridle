This is a story about a teacher.

Not the kind who just explains grammar and disappears. A different kind.

🏆

The Coach

In Korea, I ran a learning center. Out of 450,000 tutors on the country's largest platform, I was ranked 170th.

Top 0.03%

📞

The Extra Mile

I didn't just teach. Late at night, I talked to worried parents. I convinced anxious mothers that rankings weren't everything — that their child's health and happiness mattered more.

😔

The Weight

I carried every student's worries. Their mentality, their fears, their family pressures. More than any tutor should.

Because I genuinely cared.

💨

March 2025

I stepped away. Completely burnt out.

Not from working too hard — from caring too much.

✈️

The First Step

For the first time in my life, I just started traveling. No plan. No deadline. Just me and a backpack.

💬

The Questions

“Why do Koreans always ask about age?”

“Why does the verb come at the very end?”

“Why do you write addresses backwards?”

🌍

The Curiosity

It wasn't just K-Pop or K-Drama. People wanted to understand how Koreans think. The logic behind the language. The culture behind the words.

💡

The Spark

As I explained, I felt something I hadn't felt in years. Joy. Purpose.

“This is what I'm meant to do.”

📱

The Videos

But then people showed me things. Cheap, clickbait videos spreading wrong information about Korean culture. Made for attention, not understanding.

🙅

The Ignorance

People who never tried to understand. They judged from their narrow view and spoke out loud to the world. Their ignorance became someone else's “knowledge.”

⚠️

The Bigger Problem

I saw it everywhere. Not just about Korea. People worldwide — thinking in black and white. Not trying to understand each other.

🔥

The Mission

I will protect the real Korea. And build something that helps people understand each other better.

💻

The Market

When I looked at online tutoring, I was frustrated. Some tutors in pajamas. Weekly replies. Unstructured chatting at premium prices.

😢

The Damage

I met students who had been made to feel ashamed by their previous tutors. Their confidence was broken before they even began.

💪

The Promise

You deserve more than a chat partner. You deserve someone who takes your goals as seriously as you do.

🏗️

The System

I didn't just start teaching. I built a system. Because the best teacher is the one who eventually makes themselves unnecessary.

🔍

Step 1: The Gap

Where are you now? Where do you want to go? Not a grammar test — a real conversation about your life and goals.

🔄

Step 2: The Daily Routine

Class time is for verification. Real growth happens between classes. Small tasks, gentle nudges, detailed feedback within 24 hours.

🎯

Step 3: The Safe Space

Mistakes are treasure. The more we find in class, the fewer you make in real life.

No shame. Only growth.

🌐

Beyond 1:1

But I dreamed of something bigger. Not just me teaching you. But all of us learning together.

🤝

The Group

Students helping students. Explaining, listening, understanding each other. Not just learning Korean — learning to connect.

🏘️

The Community

Same timezone, same city. Study partners become travel buddies. Online connections become real friendships.

🌏

우리들

All of us. Every culture. Every language.
Understanding each other better.
